Lyra on mobile

The same wallet, now in your pocket.

This is the real Lyra wallet, the one in the Chrome extension, running on mainnet. Same colours, same layout, same Lyra. It installs on your phone straight from the browser, no store needed.

Install it

  1. iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.
Open the app

Made for touch

Left edge
Swipe right: contacts, network, chart, help, settings
Right edge
Swipe left: send, receive, swap, bridge and stake history
Top
Swipe down: every asset with its value
Wallet
Tap it, or swipe back, to bring it back

Here, with a mouse: click and drag across the wallet, like a finger.

Good to know

Keys
Encrypted on the device, never sent anywhere
Backup
Save your recovery phrase: a phone that runs out of space may clear a web app's data
Ledger
On a computer for now (USB from Chrome)
